A short introduction to planning your third age.

The earlier, the better.

Most people only plan when a crisis forces it: a diagnosis, a fall, a hospital form with a signature line and no words ready. Epilogue Life is made to be started early, while your memory is sharp and your voice is strong, so everything that matters is captured calmly, completely, and in your own words.

✦

What you get

Everything a thoughtful plan needs, held in one private folder on your own machine.

35 guided questions

Thoughtful questions across seven areas of life: care, legal, healthcare, relationships, fulfilment, arrangements, digital legacy. Answer at your own pace, skip anything.

Your profile and your people

A gentle onboarding builds who you are and who this plan serves: the profile, the loved ones, the values everything else hangs on.

A few questions a morning

A gentle daily rhythm asks two or three questions and saves your answers. Not overwhelming, never chasing. The plan quietly completes itself.

Wish I had said

Guided letters and messages for the people you love, in your voice, with notes for when each one should be read.

Last days, handled with care

Comfort and dignity preferences for the final chapter, captured gently and precisely: where, who, what matters, what to avoid.

Your life, reflected

A reflection woven from your own answers, and memory playlists that capture the soundtrack of your life, moment by moment.

The Epilogue Life Plan

The finished document: a complete, beautifully printable plan you can hold, sign, keep, and hand to the people it is for.

Share by email or in hand

Send the plan, or a single letter, through your own Gmail with one confirmed click. Or print it and place it in the drawer that matters.

Private by design

Everything stays in your folder, on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded, tracked or synced. Run it fully offline if you like.

A gentle hand when it is hard

In grief or a hard moment the agent stops, cares, and points to real people and real helplines. An assistant, never a pretend companion.

How it works

Four small steps, then a gentle rhythm that does the rest.

Step one

Install Hermes Agent

One command on any laptop, then chat from the app you already use. Hermes is free and open.

Step two

Get the agent

Copy the Epilogue Life repo into your Hermes profile. It is free, and yours to keep and change.

Step three

Onboard gently

A warm conversation builds your profile: who you are, who you love, what matters. Then the questions begin.

Step four

Answer a little, often

Two or three questions a morning, saved as you go. When you are ready, the plan is built and shared.

Free, and it stays that way

The companion itself costs nothing: the agent is source-available and free to use, the repo is the download, and your data never leaves your machine.

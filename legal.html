Privacy

Your plan lives with you. The Epilogue Life agent runs on your own machine and keeps everything in your own folder. Your answers, letters, plans and playlists are not uploaded to us, and we cannot see them. You can move, back up or delete the folder at any time.

This website collects no accounts and no personal information. It is a static site. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and a video from YouTube, which receive the requests your browser sends them, as with any site embedding their services.

Sharing is your choice, in the moment. If you connect an email account (for example through Maton) to send your plan to a loved one, the send happens from your own account, to a recipient you name and confirm. We never receive a copy.
